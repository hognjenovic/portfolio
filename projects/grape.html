FullMoon is a simple, end-to-end tool that helps vineyards track production from harvest to bottling in one place.

About the project

A small team is building a mobile app to help small wineries run more smoothly, think more clearly, and still have time for what matters: the wine itself.

I am one of the product designers on the team, design-owning all flows and features. The team is two designers, an oenologist, a business lead, and two developers.

The challenge

Small vineyards were overwhelmed by fragmented tools: spreadsheets for harvest tracking, paper logs for tank movements, and email chains for team updates.

The founders needed a single mobile app that captured their hands-on workflow without forcing rigid enterprise software on them. Balancing simple data entry during fieldwork with powerful analytics for yield decisions proved tricky.
