Design System

The colours, type, spacing and components the portfolio is built from. Every value on this page is a variable at the top of css/styles.css, so changing a token there updates this page and the site together.

Colour Typography Spacing Shape Layout Components

Colour

One blue in three steps does all the brand work. Primary is used for headlines, labels and hover states; the hero blends all three, from deep behind the headline to bright at the far corner. Everything else is white, near-black and two greys.

  • Primary · #000BAB--color-primaryHeadlines, labels, row hover, hero and 404 background
  • Primary bright · #1B1FD2--color-primary-brightFooter; lightest corner of the hero
  • Primary deep · #000550--color-primary-deepDark shade behind the hero headline
  • Shell · #1A1A1A--color-shellCase study top band and footer on every page
  • Primary tint · #F5F5FF--color-primary-tintTile backgrounds
  • Ink · #0A0A0A--color-inkBody text, titles
  • Ink soft · #333333--color-ink-softSecondary text in lists
  • Ink faint · #6B6B6B--color-ink-faintFact labels, notes and captions
  • Muted · #B8B8B8--color-mutedInactive controls
  • Line · #EBEBEB--color-lineDividers
  • Background · #FFFFFF--color-bgPage
  • On primary soft · #EDEDED--color-on-primary-softSecondary text on blue

Themes

Pages swap the accent colours with a class on <body>. The shell (hero on the homepage, case study top band, footer) is neutral on every page. Each theme sets a primary colour for small text and highlighted blocks, a display colour for type 24px and larger, a highlight colour for small details, and a tint for panels. Case study panels use the neutral grey.

  • .theme-neutralHomepage and 404 · #1A1A1A, tint #F3F3F1
  • .theme-jobcloudBlackberry #321E50 for headings, accents and highlighted blocks · Raspberry #FA463C as the highlight: heading bars, key numbers, row hover
  • Default (no class)Blue #000BAB · tint #F5F5FF · used by Wingman AI and Grape until they get themes

Typography

One family, Stack Sans Text, in five weights. Headings are set tight (negative letter-spacing, short line-height); sizes step down at the tablet (1199px) and phone (809px) breakpoints.

Spacing

Gaps inside grids are tiny (2 and 4px) while gaps between sections are large (120px), which is what gives the layout its tight-blocks-in-open-space feel.
